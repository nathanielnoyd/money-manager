# Code of Conduct

We want Money Manager to be a welcoming project for people of every background and level of experience. Personal finance can be a sensitive subject; treat it, and each other, with care.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and receive feedback about code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never ask others to share their financial details, and don't share anyone's personal information without their consent.
- Help newcomers find their footing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information, including financial information.
- Sexualized language or attention, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and to representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban participants who violate it.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository's profile, or with GitHub's private reporting feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will act fairly and proportionately, from a private warning to a permanent ban.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
